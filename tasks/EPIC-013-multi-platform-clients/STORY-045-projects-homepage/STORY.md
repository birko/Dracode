# Web UI: projects homepage overview

## User story

As a KoboldLair user, I want the homepage to tell me at a glance how all my projects are doing and what is waiting on me, so that I know where to go next without opening each project.

## Behaviour

- What needs you across projects, with a pill in the header when you are inside another project (D10)
- Cross-project charts and numbers (D18)
- A home Dragon for starting projects and cross-project questions (D12)
