# Web UI: notifications and decision cards

## User story

As a KoboldLair user, I want one notification stream I see wherever I am, and agent questions as cards I can answer in Dragon, so that nothing waiting on me gets missed.

## Behaviour

- One stream shown as a project badge, the header bell and a decision card in Dragon (D17)
- Questions agents cannot resolve arrive as answerable cards; a new card does not expand Dragon (D14, D15)
